# Key design decision for public verification of OwlAPI’s npm audit gate

**Q2 · Accepted by the owner for the first rollout · 26 November 2026 · `zero-one-zero`**

**Scope.**
This decision defines who may attest that an exact OwlAPI tarball and its observed dependency graph satisfied the advisory policy at the recorded audit time.

**Accepted decision — option B.**
The owner amended retention on 26 November 2026.
Use the existing OwlAPI repository, retain compact release evidence, and use Actions logs for routine diagnostics.
A separate evaluator repository is outside the selected design.
An operator may start a protected policy evaluator on demand for the first rollout.
The evaluator authenticates its inputs, applies the approved policy and signs its own result.
Routine raw npm output is transient.
Detailed originals are privately retained for an exception, investigation or explicit obligation.
The public seal verifies the disclosure-cleared statement against the expected evaluator, tarball, recipient graph, policy and audit observation time.
The release owner retains authority to approve publication.

During an evaluator outage, a previously issued receipt can satisfy the advisory gate if it still meets the applicable trust and freshness rules for the exact subject.
A new graph, a stale observation or a missing qualifying result holds the seal pending a successful evaluation.
Only the protected evaluator can supply the advisory verdict.

**Three different assurances matter.**
A public consumer can verify who signed an eligible statement for the artifact and graph they are checking.
An authorised reviewer with the retained report, native exit status and policy revision can reapply the historical decision.
Independently reproducing the scanner’s historical findings requires the original advisory inputs or an appropriately licensed frozen dataset, the evaluated graph, scanner, settings and matching rules.
Running `npm audit` later gives a new observation.
npm calculates indirect vulnerabilities from dependency ranges and available package versions as well as advisory records.

## Options and trade-offs

Every option must respect the applicable disclosure rights and match the decision to the exact release subject and observed graph.
For a repeatable, irreversible release, the next priority is protecting the verdict against error or substitution by the person or job requesting publication.
Manual effort, service availability and setup cost determine how to deliver that assurance.
A contractual requirement for independent assessment or public replay changes the choice.

| Option                                                                    | What the consumer trusts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Operational cost and when to choose it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A. Owner evaluates and signs**                                          | A named, authorised person attests that they obtained the authentic report, applied the specified policy and retained the original. The public signature authenticates that judgment.                                                                                     | Least initial engineering. Each release depends on that person’s availability and consistency, including after npm approval if a new graph appears. Defensible for a deliberately accepted first-party assurance level with an auditable procedure and retrievable evidence. Name the signer, scope, review or end date and required evidence in advance. If the roles coincide, evaluation and release authority are concentrated in the signer. A real denial or missing original still holds the release.                                                                                                                                       |
| **B. Operator starts a protected evaluator (selected for first rollout)** | An authorised workload obtains or authenticates the evidence, selects a trusted policy, calculates the decision and can sign only its own output. The operator supplies an immutable release reference. The public seal checks the expected issuer, workload and subject. | Requires a protected execution path, a verifiable workload identity and private handling of any exceptional retained reports. It can run on demand. It avoids hand-scoring every report and provides a repeatable first rollout. If the operator can change the rule or sign arbitrary text under the same identity, the assurance drops to A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **C. Unattended private evaluation**                                      | The same evidence contract as B, triggered by trusted CI or release events. An evaluator administered independently of the publisher can also reduce the risk of publisher override.                                                                                      | Adds integration, availability, access management and recovery work. Choose it when handoffs delay releases or coverage spans many time-sensitive graphs. Independence depends on who administers the verifier, policy and signing identity. An outside assessor needs authority to access the private data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **D. Independently replayable public assessment**                         | Anyone can run a published matcher on the exact graph and an archived, redistributable advisory snapshot. For example, the GitHub Advisory Database has a CC BY 4.0 licence.                                                                                              | Choose this if downstream parties must reproduce a finding for themselves. Publish the licensed inputs with attribution, snapshot identity, matcher version, coverage and limitations. GitHub supplies data to npm audit, but a scan of a database commit has its own matching and counting semantics. Treat it as a separate public assessment. Substitution for a mandatory npm audit requires a separate policy decision. [Database licence](https://github.com/github/advisory-database/blob/main/LICENSE.md) · [Relationship to npm audit](https://github.blog/security/supply-chain-security/github-advisory-database-now-powers-npm-audit/) |

B gives this high-consequence, repeatable release gate an independently checkable **project attestation** without requiring an always-on service or an external auditor.
It still asks users to trust OwlAPI's evaluator.
If a customer requires assurance independent of OwlAPI’s administrators, select a suitably independent verifier with authorised access under C, or provide public replay under D.
If the need is faster releases, B can evolve into C with the same statement format and acceptance rule.

## What the seal verifies

Suppose the same tarball qualified with `@types/node@26.6.10` and a public install resolved `26.6.11`.
A valid signature covering only the tarball, or covering the earlier lock, is insufficient for the later graph.
The public reader obtains the expected tarball digest from the admitted release and the actual recipient graph digest from the authenticated job receipt for **that install**.
It checks both against the signed subject.
The second graph needs its own applicable evaluation.
Several observations may share a disposition when the statement explicitly covers each subject and each has matching evidence.

| Boundary                                | Required evidence under option B                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Protected collection and evaluation** | Authenticate the exact tarball, manifest, lock, graph and audit scope. Evaluate native output and exit status in the protected job. Record tool, policy and original observation time. Routine raw output is transient. Preserve original bytes privately with an owner and disposal trigger only for a documented exception, investigation or explicit obligation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Issuance and publication**            | Use a release-approved verifier identity on protected code. The release candidate and untrusted PR jobs cannot choose the policy, sign arbitrary results or access private evidence credentials. Sign a versioned project statement about the exact subject, evidence scope, policy revision, evaluator, audit observation time and policy-decision time. If disclosure limits the public time fields, the cleared predicate must still let the seal enforce its required observation age. Publish only cleared fields. Use an opaque case reference if the raw report digest itself has no disclosure clearance. Failure output, logs, caches, Actions ZIPs, Git history and public attestation services are disclosure surfaces.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Public seal**                         | Independently select the expected signer/verifier, policy, artifact, graph and freshness rule. Verify the signature and identity, subject binding, applicable scope, original run/attempt or private verification origin, observation time and accepted outcome. Apply freshness to the underlying audit observation. Re-signing old evidence cannot restart its age. A correctly signed result for another graph, a stale result, a failed audit or malformed output keeps the release pending. Check for a superseding denial or revoked signing authority as the policy requires. An evaluator outage blocks a decision that still needs a qualifying receipt. An existing eligible receipt can satisfy the advisory gate with the required trust and freshness evidence. The release owner retains authority to approve publication through the staged process. The protected evaluator supplies the advisory decision. |
| **Historical review**                   | Verify the original signed decision, exact graph, policy and original job at the decision time. A fresh audit provides current findings. An authorised reviewer can re-score the historical report only for a case that retained it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

These controls define acceptance for the selected design.
The published attestation can use an existing signed-statement format once its fields, trust configuration and disclosure are qualified.
A key, a transparency-log entry or a timestamp on its own does not establish when the audit ran or whether its dependency coverage was complete.

## Fit to the current branch and the first rollout

The `zero-one-zero` workflow lists four audit-report members in a proof ZIP.
Three contain parsed report wrappers and one carries native transport audit output.
Release admission also runs a fresh audit and hashes reserialised JSON.
The seal requires the original proof ZIP for the public inventory.
GitHub allows signed-in readers of a public repository to download its workflow artifacts.
Moving npm-derived data into a public attestation can create another durable public copy.
Restrict collection and failure logging at the first boundary, and change the proof-member contract on both the fresh and promoted routes.
[Proof upload](../../.github/workflows/qualify-package.yml) · [Admission audit](../../scripts/release-assurance.mjs) · [Seal disclosure](../../scripts/staged-release-followup.mjs) · [GitHub artifact access](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/manage-workflow-runs/download-workflow-artifacts) · [GitHub attestation visibility](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/concepts/security/artifact-attestations#how-github-generates-artifact-attestations)

OwlAPI already checks a promoted advisory observation against a 24-hour window and has a separate read-only, identity-bound signed receipt for label refusals.
Those give an implementer concrete freshness and identity-verification precedents.
The same-repository evaluator identity and end-to-end receipt verification require qualification.
Specific private custody is required only when a case calls for retaining the detailed original.
No particular cloud service, staffing increase or third-party assessor is assumed.
The existing runbook supplies an allowed disclosure pattern.

**Acceptance before enabling the gate.**
Demonstrate one genuine pass and one blocking result on known inputs.
Prove that the signed decision binds the actual graph and policy, that a substituted graph or untrusted issuer fails at sealing, and that a synthetic restricted marker stays out of public success and failure paths.
Exercise private readback independently for a case that requires retention.
Demonstrate recovery from a verifier outage by retrying the read-only evaluation against the admitted subject.
Hold the gate if an eligible verifier is unavailable and no current applicable receipt exists, or if required exceptional evidence cannot be retained.
Revisit the design when release volume makes operator availability material, an acquirer requires independent replay, or the applicable data rights or signing trust change.

**Implementation handoff.**
Carry the accepted operator-triggered evaluator through release admission, public recipient verification and sealing under DEC-005.
Demonstrate the signer, verification and recovery controls before enabling the gate.
Exercise private custody if a particular exception or retention obligation invokes it.
